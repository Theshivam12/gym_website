01

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NEOFORM Fitness & Wellness ("NEOFORM", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, protects and processes personal data provided through our website and services.

This Policy is intended to explain our privacy practices in accordance with applicable Indian data protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules.

02

Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect the following information:

Name Your name or other information used to identify you.
Contact Details Phone number and email address.
Address City, address or other location information voluntarily provided by you.
Membership Data Membership, programme or enquiry details.
Reviews Feedback, reviews and comments submitted through the website.
Technical Data Limited technical information required for website security and operation.
We do not request unnecessary passwords, banking credentials or payment-card information through ordinary website enquiry forms.
03

Purpose of Processing

Personal data may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries and contact requests.
  • Processing membership or trial requests.
  • Providing information about gym programmes.
  • Managing member and customer records.
  • Responding to reviews and feedback.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Preventing misuse and fraudulent activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.
04

Consent

Where consent is required, NEOFORM will seek consent through a clear and understandable notice before or at the time of processing, as applicable.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

05

Sharing of Personal Data

We may share personal data with trusted service providers or data processors where reasonably necessary to provide and maintain our services.

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Database and cloud service providers.
  • Email and communication providers.
  • Security and technical service providers.
  • Professional advisers where necessary.
  • Government authorities where legally required.
NEOFORM does not sell personal data as a product or commodity.
06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption where applicable.
  • Restricted administrative access.
  • Secure password hashing.
  • Database access controls.
  • Input validation.
  • Protection against common web attacks.
  • Security updates where reasonably practicable.
No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information.
07

Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, or for another lawful purpose.

When personal data is no longer required and there is no lawful reason to retain it, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or dispose of it.

08

Subject to applicable law and conditions, you may have rights concerning your personal data.

Access

Request information about your personal data and its processing.

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Erasure

Request deletion of personal data where applicable.

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Grievance

Raise a grievance regarding personal data processing.

Nomination

Exercise nomination rights where provided by applicable law.

09

Withdrawal of Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may request withdrawal by contacting NEOFORM using the contact information provided below.

Following a valid withdrawal request, processing based on that consent will stop unless continued processing is permitted or required by applicable law.

10

Children's Data

NEOFORM does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children except where permitted by applicable law.

Where additional safeguards or verifiable parental consent are required by applicable law, NEOFORM will follow those requirements.

11

Cookies

If non-essential tracking or analytics technologies are introduced, appropriate notice and consent mechanisms will be provided where required.

12
